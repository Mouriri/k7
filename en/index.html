Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a private K-7 VTC chauffeur?

Booking is very simple! You can contact us directly via WhatsApp for an immediate response, or call us. We will send you a fixed quote immediately.

Are your prices fixed or do they depend on traffic?

Unlike traditional taxis, our prices are 100% fixed upon booking. Traffic jams or deviations will never impact the final price of your journey.

Do you accept credit card payments?

Yes, all our vehicles are equipped with payment terminals accepting major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) as well as contactless payment and cash.

Do your drivers speak English?

Absolutely. Our professional chauffeurs are bilingual (French/English) to ensure perfect communication with our international clientele.
