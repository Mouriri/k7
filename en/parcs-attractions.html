Why choose a VTC for your trips to amusement parks?

We know that traveling with children requires organization. That is why our premium sedans and vans are perfectly adapted to families. When booking, let us know the age of your children, and we will provide the necessary car seats or booster seats free of charge to ensure their optimal safety.

No driving fatigue or parking stress

Spending an entire day at an amusement park is exhausting. With K-7 VTC, no one needs to sacrifice themselves to drive on the way back. Your private chauffeur drops you off directly at the park entrance in the morning and picks you up at the same place in the evening. No more exorbitant parking fees or endless lines to get out of the parking lots!

Transparent pricing for a controlled budget

Family outings represent a significant budget. With our service, the price of your transfer is 100% fixed and communicated when you book. No luggage fees, no surcharge for strollers, and no unpleasant surprises even in case of traffic jams on the highway.
