Private VTC Sightseeing and Cultural Tours

Discover the richness of French heritage in the utmost comfort. K-7 VTC offers you a dedicated private chauffeur service for your sightseeing tours from Paris. Forget crowded tourist buses with strict schedules, and enjoy a tailor-made visit to the most emblematic places in France, such as the Palace of Versailles, the gardens of Giverny, or the majestic Mont-Saint-Michel.

Why rent a VTC for your sightseeing tours?

A personalized tour at your own pace

Travel without constraints. Your excursion fully adapts to your desires. Want to spend more time in a museum, take a detour for a photo shoot, or change your route along the way? Your driver is entirely at your disposal to offer you a unique and flexible experience.

Absolute comfort for long journeys

Some of France's most beautiful wonders are located a few hours' drive from Paris. Aboard our high-end vehicles (sedans or spacious vans), enjoy a relaxing journey with air conditioning, leather seats, Wi-Fi, and refreshing bottled water. The journey becomes an integral part of your leisure trip.

A discreet and helpful chauffeur

Our drivers know the tourist routes perfectly. They drop you off as close as possible to the monument entrances to save you unnecessary walking. Professional and discreet, they ensure your safety and that of your personal belongings left in the vehicle during your visits.

Our must-see excursions from Paris

The Palace of Versailles and its gardens

Immerse yourself in the history of France and the lives of its kings. Located a few dozen kilometers from Paris, the Palace of Versailles is a must-see. Your driver will take you to the palace gates and wait for you while you visit the Hall of Mirrors and stroll through the sumptuous gardens designed by André Le Nôtre.

Giverny and the Claude Monet Foundation

Let yourself be charmed by Normandy. In Giverny, discover Claude Monet's house and the famous water gardens that inspired his Water Lilies paintings. A peaceful getaway, ideal for a half-day trip, with direct transport from your Parisian hotel.

A magical day at Mont-Saint-Michel

Live an unforgettable experience by visiting this UNESCO World Heritage marvel. Although Mont-Saint-Michel is located far from the capital, our long-distance VTC service guarantees you a smooth and comfortable ride for a full-day excursion full of discoveries.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the driver wait for us during the visit?

Absolutely. Depending on the chosen package (hourly booking), your driver remains on-site or nearby to watch the vehicle and pick you up as soon as you finish your visit.

Can we customize our tour itinerary?

Yes, our excursions are 100% tailor-made. You can add stops (like a restaurant break) or combine several nearby locations on the same day.

Are meals and entrance tickets included?

No, our service only covers premium transport and the driver's service. The purchase of monument entrance tickets and meals remains at your expense.
