Hourly VTC Chauffeur Service for Events: Weddings, Fashion Week, and Seminars

Need a vehicle and a private chauffeur entirely at your disposal for a few hours or for the whole day? Hourly booking (Mise à disposition) is the ideal premium solution for your special events and intense professional travels in Paris and the Île-de-France region. Forget the stress of ordering a vehicle at each stage of your day: with K-7 VTC, your driver faithfully waits for you.

The advantages of our hourly booking service

Total flexibility for your travels

Are your business meetings back-to-back or is your shopping session running late? With hourly booking, you are the master on board. Your private driver remains nearby and is ready to take you to your next destination at the slightest request, without any waiting time.

A prestigious image for your events

Arriving in a luxury sedan or a premium Van adds an undeniable touch of elegance. Whether to impress an important client, for the arrival of the bride and groom, or for transporting celebrities, the allure of our vehicles and the suited professionalism of our drivers reinforce your brand image.

Hourly or daily pricing

Unlike standard trips, this service is billed by the hour (often with a mileage allowance included). You thus have perfect control over your event budget, without worrying about unforeseen waiting fees or last-minute surcharges.

Our tailor-made event services

The best day of your life deserves exceptional transport. We handle the transfer of the bride and groom in a prestigious vehicle, as well as the shuttle for your guests between the ceremony venue, the reception hall, and their hotels at the end of the night. Trust us with your wedding logistics.

Transport for Paris Fashion Week

Fashion Week imposes a frantic pace. Fashion shows, private parties, fittings... Our drivers know Parisian shortcuts and ensure the transport of VIPs, models, and production teams with the utmost discretion, speed, and efficiency.

Seminars, Congresses, and Trade Shows

Whether you are going to Paris Expo Porte de Versailles or the Villepinte Exhibition Center (VIPARIS), make traveling easier for your collaborators or distinguished guests. We coordinate fleets of vans to ensure smooth shuttles throughout your exhibition.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is the price of an hourly booking calculated?

The price is determined based on the duration (number of hours) and the type of vehicle chosen (Sedan or Van). A number of kilometers is included in the basic package, which largely covers intra-muros trips and nearby suburbs.

Can we decorate the vehicle for a wedding?

Absolutely! We can connect you with our partner florists or let you decorate the door handles and the rear parcel shelf of the vehicle (provided the decorations do not scratch the paintwork).

Can you transport large groups for a seminar?

Yes, we are used to managing the logistics of large corporate events. By notifying us in advance, we can mobilize several Vans and coordinate all the transport for your teams.
